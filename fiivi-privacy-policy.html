Your planner and account

Fiivi stores your planner on your device and saves an account copy through Supabase. After setting up and saving a new plan on your device, you must sign in with Apple or Google before starting a trial, subscribing, or using Fiivi’s main features. Your Fiivi account uses your email address and account identifier; provider account metadata may include your name. Fiivi does not receive your Apple or Google password.

While signed in, changes can be sent automatically to your account. The saved copy can include routine, activity and habit titles, notes, steps, dates, recurring plans, scheduling preferences, habit checkoffs, completion history, equipped companion appearance and calendar activities you chose to import. Scheduling quiet hours and activity reminders can be included. The device’s global notification enablement, motion and privacy preferences, and calendar source identifiers stay local. Active timers, unfinished setup, undo state and inactive compatibility records are excluded from the account copy.

Normal changes within the same Fiivi account merge automatically. When you switch to a known different account, Fiivi restores that account’s saved plan or starts fresh setup if it has no saved plan, preserving the previous local plan in a private recovery copy on the device. Finish or stop an active routine before switching accounts. Signing out leaves your local planner and the cloud account copy intact. Subscription expiry alone does not erase your planner.

To restore planning progress on another device, sign into the same Fiivi account. Check the account status in You before relying on the saved cloud copy. If Fiivi asks you to choose between saved plans during recovery, review the choices carefully. Offline changes need a successful connection before reaching another device. An active timer stays on its original device. Sync depends on connectivity, service availability and successful account checks; an account copy does not guarantee that every version of a plan can be recovered.

Sign-in and service providers

Fiivi uses Apple’s native sign-in and Google’s sign-in SDK to authenticate your account. Supabase provides account authentication and stores your saved account plan and subscription records in the configured United States region, West US / North California. Apple handles subscription payments; Fiivi does not receive your payment-card number.

Google may process account identifiers and an IP address to estimate general location for fraud prevention. Its bundled sign-in SDK also declares account, device and usage information for functionality and analytics, including name, email address, phone number, user and device identifiers, coarse location, other usage data and other data types. Fiivi does not ask you for a phone number or request device location permission. Fiivi does not operate a separate planner analytics or advertising service. Providers process service and security information under their applicable policies.

See Apple’s privacy policy, Google’s privacy policy, Google’s sign-in SDK disclosure and Supabase’s privacy policy. Fiivi does not request contacts, camera, microphone or HealthKit access.

Optional device permissions and shared surfaces

If you choose to connect a calendar, iOS asks for full calendar access. Fiivi reads upcoming timed events in a 14-day window and lets you choose what to import. All-day events are excluded from that list. Imported activities are planning copies that may be included in account sync. Fiivi does not modify source calendars or continuously synchronize imported copies with their original events. Revoke access in iOS Settings to stop new access. Remove previously imported activities separately if you no longer want those copies.

Fiivi schedules local reminders using planning details and timing preferences. It has no developer-operated remote push service. Notification text may appear on your Lock Screen or connected Apple devices. Control permissions and previews in iOS Settings. Quiet hours and device notification settings can prevent alerts.

When enabled and supported, Live Activities show routine steps, timers and progress on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. Fiivi updates them locally using Apple’s ActivityKit and App Intents; it has no Live Activity push server. Dismissing a Live Activity does not delete the routine.

Home Screen widgets use a small device-local copy shared with Fiivi’s widget extension: upcoming activity titles and times, routine status, the active step and equipped companion appearance. It excludes planner notes, account identity and sign-in tokens. Widgets can show personal details on your Home Screen. Hide routine details hides titles in widgets, Live Activities and local reminders; it does not erase screenshots or other copies you share. Signing out or losing access replaces the shared widget copy with an unavailable state when Fiivi processes the change. iOS controls widget refresh and notification delivery.

Apple subscriptions and recovery

Fiivi sends signed Apple purchase information to its subscription service and verifies current status with Apple. The service stores the purchased product, original purchase identifier, account association, purchase environment, access and renewal status, and relevant dates. These records support subscription access, renewals, refunds, revocations and protection against purchase reassignment.

Restore Purchases checks Apple subscription access; it does not recover a deleted planner. Recovering a currently active purchase from a deleted Fiivi account requires fresh matching Apple Account proof and current verification. A purchase belonging to a living Fiivi account cannot be transferred this way. A restricted recovery audit retains the prior purchase association, destination account identifiers, verification time and a hash of the recovery proof. The audit does not store the signed recovery receipt text.

Apple processes payments and maintains its purchase records under its own policies. Apple may process App Store use, diagnostics and crash information according to its policies and your settings, and provide permitted reports to developers. This processing is separate from Fiivi’s saved planner and subscription records.

Deletion, retained records and backups

Use You → Your Fiivi account → Delete account to request deletion. Fiivi requires fresh Face ID, Touch ID or device passcode authentication; Apple-linked accounts also require fresh Apple authorization. Cancelling device authentication, or being unable to complete it, leaves the account intact. When the server confirms deletion, it removes the Fiivi account and active cloud planner copy. Fiivi then clears this device’s saved planner, history, setup, appearance and app-managed recovery copies. If local cleanup cannot finish, Fiivi reports the problem and retries when reopened.

Account deletion does not cancel your Apple subscription. Cancel renewal separately through Apple. It does not remotely erase local copies on other devices, files previously exported or shared, or external backups. Manage those copies separately. Signing in again creates a fresh account and does not restore deleted plans. Restoring subscription access after deletion does not restore deleted planning data. Signing out is separate from deleting an account.

Purchase ownership and recovery-audit records remain after account deletion to verify subscription ownership and prevent reassignment. Account deletion does not automatically purge those records, provider operational logs or provider-held copies. Fiivi does not promise immediate removal from every provider system. Contact getfiivi@gmail.com for a privacy or deletion request concerning retained information; we will explain any applicable retention limits or requirements.

Apple’s device backups and migration may include app data depending on your settings. Fiivi does not operate or guarantee those services. File export is not available in the account-enabled version. When evaluating a beta, use fictional planning entries and do not make it the sole copy of important information.

Support information

Contact getfiivi@gmail.com for help. Support information may include your email address, any name supplied, your message and attachments. Google and your email provider handle delivery. Check Inbox and Spam for replies; delivery and a response are not guaranteed. You can ask a general support question without sending your planner or private notes. Do not send passwords, verification codes, sign-in tokens, payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive information.

We use received support information to answer your request, investigate the issue, maintain necessary service and security records, and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not sell support information or use it for unrelated marketing or targeted advertising.

The documented procedure retains open cases and reviews resolved cases for removal in monthly cleanup after 90 days. Information needed for a continuing issue, necessary security records or an applicable legal obligation may be retained longer. Provider retention is separate; this is not a promise of automatic mailbox or provider deletion on a particular day. You may ask us to delete information you sent to support. We will review the request and explain information that must be retained and why.

Security and younger users

Fiivi uses iOS app storage protections, account access controls and Apple transaction verification. Protect your device and take care with screenshots and Lock Screen previews. No storage or communication method is guaranteed completely secure.

Fiivi is intended for teens and adults aged 13 and over. People under 13 are not permitted to create or use a Fiivi account. Fiivi does not ask for your age or date of birth during onboarding. An App Store content rating and parental download controls are separate from this service age policy and do not verify every user’s age. Teen privacy and any parent or guardian permission required by applicable law still apply. If you believe we hold personal information from an under-13 user, contact getfiivi@gmail.com so we can investigate and address it under applicable law. Avoid sending a child’s planner or sensitive details unless necessary for the request.
