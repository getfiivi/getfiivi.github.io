A little help with Fiivi

Fiivi is provided by Anthony Dale Pancho, California, United States. For app support or privacy requests, email getfiivi@gmail.com.

Something isn't working?

Tell us what happened, what you expected, and your device model, iOS/iPadOS version and Fiivi version. A screenshot may help; hide private planning details first. Do not send passwords, sign-in tokens, payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive information. Send only the details needed to explain the issue; you do not need to share your complete planner. No particular response time is promised.

Manage a subscription

In iPhone or iPad Settings, tap your name → Subscriptions → Fiivi. You can also follow Apple's cancellation instructions or request an eligible refund from Apple. Deleting Fiivi, its planning data or your Fiivi account does not cancel a subscription. Applicable consumer rights still apply.

Restore access or your plan

Restore Purchases checks subscription access with Apple. Sign into the intended Fiivi account before restoring. It does not restore deleted planning data. Recovery of an active purchase from a deleted Fiivi account requires fresh matching Apple Account proof and current Apple verification; a purchase belonging to a living Fiivi account cannot be transferred this way.

To restore your saved planner on another device, sign into the same Fiivi account. Changes within that account merge automatically when connectivity and account checks succeed. Check the account status in You before relying on the saved cloud copy. Review any recovery choice before replacing a plan. Finish or stop an active routine before switching accounts; its timer stays on the originating device. Signing out leaves your saved plan intact.

File export is unavailable in the account-enabled version. Apple's device backups and migration are separate services. An account copy or device backup does not guarantee that every version of a plan can be restored. When evaluating a beta, use fictional entries and do not make it the sole copy of important information.

Delete your account or ask about privacy

Use You → Your Fiivi account → Delete account. Fresh device authentication is required; Apple-linked accounts also require fresh Apple authorization. After the server confirms deletion, Fiivi removes your account and active cloud planner copy and clears this device's saved planner, history, setup, appearance and app-managed recovery copies. If local cleanup cannot finish, Fiivi reports the problem and retries when reopened. Signing in again creates a fresh account; subscription recovery does not recover deleted plans.

Deletion does not cancel Apple billing or remotely erase local copies on other devices, previously saved/shared files or external backups. Purchase ownership and recovery-audit records remain for subscription ownership checks and protection against reassignment; provider logs and provider-held copies are not automatically purged. See the Privacy Policy for retained information and contact us about a privacy request. Removing the app alone does not delete the cloud account or cancel billing.

Email getfiivi@gmail.com about information you sent to support or a privacy concern. See the Privacy Policy for handling and choices, and Terms of Use for subscriptions and the Apple app license.
